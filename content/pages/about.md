I grew up in Patan, in the Kathmandu Valley, and I live in Sydney now. Both places matter to me,
which is a harder thing to explain than it sounds.

I work in technology. I studied Information Technology, spent an internship building address and
property tooling, and I keep building things because that is still the part I enjoy most — the
point where something you assembled starts actually working.

Outside of that: I run long distances, badly and stubbornly. I play chess and lose pieces I should
have seen. I read about space more than is strictly useful, and I still find it genuinely difficult
to look at the numbers involved without feeling something. I watch films and then think about them
for weeks.

None of that fits neatly into a portfolio, which is roughly why this site exists. RESON is where
those things live in one place, on a domain I own, instead of scattered across platforms that could
disappear or change their minds.

If you are here to look at my work, [that's through here](/work/). Otherwise, have a
[look around](/explore/).
